Website Overhaul

We completely revamped the BrightMinds Academy website, focusing on a clean, professional design that reflects their educational values. The new layout made it easier for students to navigate the courses and access educational resources.

Improved Course Discovery

We introduced advanced course filtering and a search functionality that allowed prospective students to easily find the courses that fit their needs. This enhanced discovery led to a significant uptick in course registrations.

User-Centered Design

The new website design focused on improving the user experience by ensuring the interface was intuitive and easy to use. With streamlined navigation and better calls to action, students could register for courses with minimal friction.

Ongoing Support and Updates

Post-launch, we worked with BrightMinds Academy to refine the website’s features, implement user feedback, and introduce new functionalities like student progress tracking. This ensured their platform continued to evolve in line with user needs.
