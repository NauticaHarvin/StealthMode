What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over time.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to improve the performance of our website, personalize content and ads, provide social media features, and analyze our traffic. By continuing to use our site, you consent to our use of cookies.

How to Manage Cookies

You can control the use of cookies through your browser settings. You can block or delete cookies, but please note that some website features may not function properly without them.
